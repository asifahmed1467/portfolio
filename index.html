Why LoRA is cheap

Full fine-tuning updates every weight in a layer. LoRA freezes the layer and trains two thin matrices (A and B) of rank r instead. Move the sliders.

Full fine-tune
LoRA (A and B)

LoRA trains only of the weights in this layer.

full = d × d  ·  LoRA = d × r + r × d

What quantization saves

My thesis studies quantized Bangla LLMs. Fewer bits per weight means less memory, so bigger models fit on smaller hardware. Pick a size and a precision.

for the weights alone

memory ≈ parameters × bits ÷ 8  ·  ignores activations and the KV cache

Smaller does not come free: quality, script handling and energy use can change. That is exactly what the thesis measures.
